Subramaniam Murugesan

PhD Researcher, School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science
Research Technician, Blizard Institute
Queen Mary University of London

I work on RF localisation and machine learning for wireless capsule endoscopy. My PhD investigates how radio measurements can tell us where a capsule is. It is funded by an EPSRC studentship and supervised by Professor Akram Alomainy and Dr Mohamed Adhnan Thaha in the Antennas and Electromagnetics Research Group.

At the Blizard Institute, I work on CE-Track, using AI to reconstruct and model clinical imaging data.
